# Guest checkout

As a new shopper, I want to complete checkout without creating an account so that I can buy quickly.

## Acceptance criteria

- The shopper enters an email and delivery address.
- The order summary shows items, delivery fee, tax, and final total.
- Card details are validated before the order is submitted.
- A payment failure keeps the entered address and shows a retry option.
- A successful order shows a unique confirmation number.

## Notes

- Quantity limits and supported countries are not defined yet.
- The confirmation is also sent by email.
